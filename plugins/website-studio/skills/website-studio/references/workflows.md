# Website Studio workflows

## Mode and output contract
- **Create:** brief/constraints → deliberate visual direction → working local site → measured verification. Reuse the supplied brief. Match the existing project stack; for a new project choose the smallest appropriate stack, not React by default. Build real pages/content/interactions, not a decorative stub.
- **Analyse:** target/scope/conditions → observed evidence → severity-ranked findings → bounded remediation plan. Browser/read-only by default. Each finding has page/viewport, reproducible observation, impact, recommendation, evidence path and confidence. Label heuristic design judgement separately from measured defects. Do not turn an audit into source edits without authority.
- **Refine:** preserve identity and architecture → targeted improvements → regression checks. Prioritize user tasks, hierarchy, typography, content clarity, accessibility and speed before decorative effects.
- **Redesign:** record baseline contracts → new visual direction → implement → compare. Preserve factual content, URLs/redirects/anchors, metadata, forms, consent behaviour, conversion events and analytics payloads. Scope real product changes separately from cosmetic redesign.

## Intake proportional to work
Record audience, primary action, brand assets/tone, content ownership, stack, target pages and edit/deploy authority. Existing brief/approved direction is sufficient; do not repeat questionnaires or create new approval pauses for ordinary authorized local work. For materially different cost, destructive scope or customer-facing effect, use the user's approved controls.

## Composition order
Project brief and existing contracts win. Impeccable diagnoses and selects quality passes; Taste supplies deliberate art direction. React Bits supplies only fitting components. When the owner explicitly selects GSAP, Lenis or a React Bits component, implement that choice rather than defaulting to CSS or arguing for native-only motion; otherwise GSAP and Lenis remain optional and must earn their lifecycle cost. Preserve accessibility, brand and progressive enhancement. Existing adapters are curated entry points, not asserted current mirrors; inspect the relevant pinned primary source before adapting it.

For an explicit motion direction, do not stop at a proposal, dependency installation or an assigned task. Implement and exercise the integration in the isolated candidate, then show real browser motion and fallback evidence before calling it delivered. Use Taste for the composed visual direction and Impeccable to critique rendered pixels; component names/imports are not design proof. Treat the requested tools as an intentional stack choice, not a mandate to add every related library or template. When the owner has explicitly said to proceed after confirming the direction, move directly into implementation: that instruction authorizes the ordinary local candidate, tests, preview and bounded visual refinement already in scope, so do not dispatch another audit or stop at a handoff/task-created status.

For redesign motion, judge the composition, not the count of effects: give the hero a deliberate focal composition and make each scroll chapter communicate its relationship or story visually rather than merely animating paragraph entrances. At the actual narrow-mobile viewport, verify that choreography and key action survive a distinct responsive composition. If owner feedback says the design still needs more, materially recompose the hero and most important narrative scene; do not answer with longer delays or more small fades. Run an actual pixel/browser review before declaring the direction satisfied.

## Browser/site evidence checklist
1. Establish the exact source/preview identity and route scope before comparison; preserve that baseline read-only. Capture complete representative page screenshots and exact HTML/DOM receipts at desktop and narrow mobile widths, plus viewport-only screenshots to judge above-the-fold action visibility. Record conditions, route, viewport, status, console/runtime/request failures, horizontal overflow, primary CTA coordinates, and whether the source change actually appears in the target preview. Test primary actions, native navigation/anchors, forms, focus/keyboard and touch behaviour; check JavaScript-disabled use when the experience should remain server-rendered. Include reduced-motion enabled and disabled when effects exist. A first-viewport screenshot cannot establish whole-page design quality, nor does a running preview prove a candidate is deployed.
2. For numeric performance/accessibility results, run an available actual measurement tool, retain its raw output and record URL, timestamp, viewport/device/profile and lab/field conditions. A deterministic style/design detector is diagnostic input, not a performance, WCAG, or overall quality score; corroborate every actionable result against actual DOM/source or rendered pixels and discard false positives. Record cold/warm cache and throttling explicitly: viewport changes in one browser session are not fresh mobile-device benchmarks. No tool means no numeric score; a clean automated scan is not proof of WCAG compliance. Screenshot descriptions are hypotheses, not measured fonts/contrast or DOM facts; corroborate with actual readbacks. Await the intended scroll position and lazy loading before classifying a missing image or capturing a section.
3. Reconcile worker narratives with retained raw interaction samples before accepting defects: expanded/closed attributes and delayed post-transition focus can contradict an immediate snapshot. For timed opacity crossfades, compare each scene's computed opacity across normal/reduced-motion timestamps; unchanged image lists or zero active animations between transitions do not prove stalled or broken motion. Separate requested device width from actual layout viewport and document width; clipped offscreen carousel children alone do not establish horizontal page overflow. In network evidence, bind responses to request methods so successful OPTIONS/preflights are not mistaken for successful data GETs.
4. For redesigns, assess all public route templates and navigation, not only the landing hero; inspect source-only or authenticated member surfaces separately and clearly mark them unverified until actually exercised. Retain brand/product truths, required warnings, link destinations, native anchor/keyboard semantics, and strict CSP/server-rendering contracts while streamlining repeated copy. Design motion only for purposeful orientation or feedback; avoid scroll hijacking and ensure autoplay or ambient motion is justified, bounded and accessible. Do not add costly libraries by default, but do implement a library/component the owner explicitly selected unless a real compatibility or safety issue blocks it; if blocked, record the concrete reason and continue unaffected work. Retain complete static/no-JS/reduced-motion paths. For edits, run the project's actual build, type/lint and focused tests as applicable; compare routes, metadata, analytics event names/payloads and duplicates with baseline. Verify fallback with enhancement disabled and client navigation/unmount cleanup. Record specific checks not available.
5. Present before/after evidence and exact results. If the browser is down, report visual verification blocked, continue safe source/tests and do not claim visual completion. If only a URL is supplied, report findings and request source/CMS access only when edits are requested.

## Motion decision checklist
Before designing motion, inspect the actual hero/component, real imagery/content and approved brief. Identify visitor tasks, classifying routes as persuade, read or operate; one dramatic homepage idea must not dictate the treatment of utility/auth pages. For owner-selected libraries, decide how to use them within these constraints rather than treating the library choice itself as still pending. State a focal moment, continuity/feedback, and route-specific intensity. Adapt pinned template source after checking its actual SSR/JavaScript-failure behavior, accessibility, reduced-motion changes, dependency and license; starter defaults are not assumed production-safe. Preserve immediately readable server-rendered content, native controls and link targets. Scope animation setup and completely clean ticker/trigger/listener/RAF state across route transitions and preference changes. Bound motion travel/duration and prefer a coherent visual vocabulary over many stock effects.

Validate the actual rendered experience at desktop and mobile, including in-motion and settled content, keyboard/focus/native anchors, JavaScript disabled or failed enhancement, reduced motion both at load and toggled live, and component unmount/route transitions. Capture openable short video of the real sequence plus representative stills; static screenshots cannot prove a smooth-scrolling or timed-animation integration. Record asset/network and main-thread observations under the tested local conditions; do not invent frame-rate, field-vitals or user-impact claims. Show where the source/build is served and do not confuse a working isolated candidate with a deployed/live site. Treat timings as design targets, not performance measurements; never imply fictional product, event or runtime states.

Outcome first; list changed files/artefacts, measured checks, gaps and next action. Distinguish proposed, locally implemented, browser-verified and deployed. No invented testimonials, product claims, images, audit scores, CI or business outcomes.

## Apple Design interaction pass
Use the optional pinned Apple Design source for directly manipulated controls, sheets, menus, type hierarchy or depth where relevant. Keep instant press feedback separate from click/keyboard commitment; never make consequential actions fire on pointer-down. Test interruption/reversal, pointercancel/lost capture, focus return, keyboard and non-drag alternatives. Respect reduced motion, transparency and contrast preferences; provide solid/readable surfaces where effects are unsupported. Keep the chosen animation owner and design system; do not force Motion, replace GSAP or rebrand every site as Apple. Upstream numerical examples are design starting points, not measured results or universal requirements.
